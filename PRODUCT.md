# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People managing their own long-term fitness, nutrition, and strength progress,
including first responders who train at home, commercial gyms, and fire
stations with different equipment at each location. The primary workflow is
fast, one-handed use on a phone before and during a workout, with desktop access
for reviewing history and planning.

## Product Purpose

Fitness Tracker is a mobile-first training companion that combines durable
personal records, guided workouts, nutrition tracking, and an app-aware AI
Coach. Success means the user can describe a goal, show the equipment available
in the current environment, review a safe workout proposal, and begin training
without rebuilding the same context on every device.

## Brand Personality

Capable, direct, and encouraging. The product should feel like a dependable
training partner: focused during a session, clear when reviewing data, and
honest about uncertainty in AI-generated or photo-derived information.

## Anti-references

- Decorative cyber or neon styling that competes with workout data.
- Dense spreadsheet-style logging during an active session.
- Novel controls that make common camera, upload, save, or delete actions hard
  to recognize.
- AI language that overclaims what the Coach can see, has saved, or has changed.
- Medical certainty, hidden automatic mutations, or unreviewed AI workout
  actions.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the athlete in control: AI proposes and the user confirms.
2. Make the current training context visible, especially location, equipment,
   and whether media is still attached.
3. Prefer familiar mobile controls and one-handed actions during training.
4. Preserve long-term progress across devices without making connectivity a
   requirement for an active workout.
5. State uncertainty plainly and provide a quick correction path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus visibility, labels, and keyboard access.
Primary touch targets should be at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. Respect reduced
motion preferences. Camera features must retain file-upload and manual-entry
fallbacks for unsupported devices, denied permissions, and users who cannot or
do not want to use live capture.
